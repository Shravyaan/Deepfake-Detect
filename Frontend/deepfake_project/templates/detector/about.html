About Our Project

Our Mission

In an era of rapid AI advancement, distinguishing between real and synthetic media is more difficult than ever. Our mission is to provide an accessible tool that helps users verify the authenticity of images, promoting transparency and trust in digital content.

The Technology

The core of this detector is a **ResNet (Residual Neural Network)**, a powerful deep learning model. It was trained on a large dataset of both real and fake images, learning the subtle artifacts and inconsistencies that AI-generated content often leaves behind.
